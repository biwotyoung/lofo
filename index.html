About Our Lost & Found System

Our Lost & Found System is designed to help people recover their lost belongings and return found items to their rightful owners.

The system serves the entire community, including students, staff, and visitors to our campus.

How It Works:

  1. Report a lost or found item with as much detail as possible
  2. Our system matches lost and found items based on descriptions
  3. You'll be notified when a potential match is found
  4. Arrange for item return through secure communication
